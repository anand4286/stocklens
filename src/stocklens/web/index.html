Stock analytics that show their working.

Eight independent families of evidence, one transparent blend, honest risk statistics, and an AI analyst agent whose every cited number is verified against its tools.

Explainable score

Every family scores −1 to +1 or abstains. Missing evidence lowers confidence instead of posing as neutral, and contributions sum to the score.

Guarded AI agent

Claude works through read-only tools bound to one symbol, submits a schema-validated note, and each cited figure is checked against what the tools returned.

Real risk statistics

EWMA volatility, historical VaR and expected shortfall, drawdown, beta, Hurst and variance ratio — with sample floors, not numbers from three data points.

Secure by default

Strict CSP with no inline script, rate limits, constant-time key checks, non-root read-only container, and deploy templates with a firewall for every target.

StockLens is open-source educational software. Nothing here is investment advice. Apache-2.0.
